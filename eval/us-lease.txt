RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This lease is made between the Landlord and the Tenant named on the signature page, for the apartment described there.

1. Term. The term of this lease is twelve months, beginning on the start date shown on the signature page.

2. Rent. The Tenant shall pay rent of $2,400 on the first day of each month by electronic transfer to the account the Landlord names.

3. Late charge. If any rent remains unpaid for five days after it is due, the Tenant shall pay a late charge of 10 percent of the monthly rent.

4. Security deposit. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to two months' rent. The deposit will not earn interest, and the Landlord will return it within sixty days after the Tenant vacates, less any deductions the Landlord in its sole discretion considers necessary.

5. Moving out. When the lease ends, the Tenant shall pay for professional carpet cleaning and for repainting every room, whatever their condition.

6. Repairs. The Tenant accepts the apartment as is and shall pay for all repairs to the apartment, including the plumbing, heating and electrical systems.

7. Rent changes. The Landlord may increase the rent at any time during the term by giving the Tenant thirty days' written notice.

8. Entry. The Landlord and its agents may enter the apartment at any time and without notice for inspections, repairs or showings.

9. Leaving early. If the Tenant ends this lease before the end of the term, the Tenant shall remain liable for the rent for the rest of the term and the security deposit shall be forfeited.

10. Renewal. This lease renews automatically for a further twelve months unless the Tenant gives written notice at least ninety days before the end of the term.

11. Legal costs. The Tenant shall pay the Landlord's attorney's fees and costs in any dispute arising under this lease.

12. Utilities. The Tenant shall pay for the electricity and gas used in the apartment. The Landlord shall pay for water and sewer service.

13. Quiet enjoyment. The Landlord agrees that the Tenant, paying the rent and keeping the terms of this lease, may occupy the apartment without interference.

14. Governing law. This lease is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
