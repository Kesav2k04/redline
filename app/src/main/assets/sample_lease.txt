RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between the Landlord and the Tenant named in Schedule A, in respect of the residential flat described in Schedule B.

1. Term. The term of this agreement shall be eleven months commencing on the date set out in Schedule A, with a lock in period of eleven months during which the agreement may not be determined by the Tenant.

2. Rent. The rent shall be paid on or before the fifth day of each calendar month by electronic transfer to the account nominated by the Landlord.

3. Late payment. If any instalment of rent remains unpaid for three days after the due date, the Tenant shall pay an additional charge of ten percent of the monthly rent for each week or part thereof during which the default continues.

4. Security deposit. The Tenant shall deposit a sum equivalent to ten months rent, which shall be interest free and shall be refunded within ninety days of the Tenant vacating the premises, after deducting such sums as the Landlord in its sole discretion considers necessary for repainting and restoration.

5. Repairs. The Tenant shall at their own expense keep the premises including all fixtures, sanitary installations, electrical wiring and the structure in good condition. Any repair below five thousand rupees shall be borne solely by the Tenant, and the Tenant shall not set off any such amount against the rent under any circumstances.

6. Outgoings. The Tenant shall bear all outgoings including municipal taxes, property tax, society maintenance and any levy hereafter imposed by any authority in respect of the premises.

7. Escalation. The rent shall stand enhanced by ten percent at the end of each period of eleven months, and such enhancement shall operate automatically without the requirement of any further agreement.

8. Access. The Landlord or their authorised agent shall be entitled to enter the premises at any reasonable hour without prior notice for the purpose of inspection, and the Tenant shall not obstruct such entry.

9. Occupancy. No person other than those named in Schedule A shall be permitted to reside in the premises, and the introduction of any additional occupant shall attract an increase in rent of fifteen percent.

10. Determination. Termination by the Tenant shall require six months notice in writing, failing which three months rent shall be payable as liquidated damages.

11. Renewal. If the Tenant fails to communicate an intention to renew at least ninety days before expiry, this agreement shall stand automatically renewed for a further eleven months on the same terms.

12. Notices. Any notice under this agreement shall be deemed to have been served on the third day after dispatch by registered post, whether or not the same is actually received by the addressee.

13. Quiet enjoyment. The Landlord covenants that the Tenant, paying the rent and observing the terms herein, shall peacefully hold and enjoy the premises during the term without interruption by the Landlord.

14. Governing law. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at Chennai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
